**Report contract.** Results first. The first line is `STATUS: done | partly done | blocked`. Keep every header field below and the role's own fields, written as briefly as they allow, with the header field names and order unchanged. Stay inside the line cap the brief states (default 12 lines; the brief may raise it): counts, paths and PR or commit numbers replace logs, diffs and restated task text. Evidence is one line per claim (the command and its exit code); never paste more than 5 lines of output. A report that cannot fit the cap without dropping a mandatory field or a finding does not drop it: it says so and asks for a higher cap. Anything the lead must decide goes in a final line starting `NEEDS:`.
